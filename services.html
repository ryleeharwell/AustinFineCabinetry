Services

Custom Cabinetry Services

From first concept to final install, we help plan and build custom cabinetry for kitchens, bathrooms, closets, and built-ins.

Kitchen cabinetry

Full custom kitchens — cabinets, islands, and pantry storage — planned and built to fit the space and layout.

Bathroom vanities

Custom vanities built to match or complement the rest of a home's cabinetry.

Closet systems

Built-in closet storage designed around the space and how it will be used.

Built-ins & millwork

Bookcases, entertainment centers, window seats, and mudroom storage, built to fit oddly-shaped spaces exactly.

Refacing & refinishing

Update existing cabinetry with new doors, paint, or hardware without a full teardown.

Planning & quoting

We'll help develop the plans for your cabinetry and turn around an accurate quote and timeline.
