Services

What we build

Every project starts with a conversation about how you use your space — then we design and build around that.

Kitchen cabinetry

Full custom kitchens — cabinets, islands, pantry storage — designed around your layout and how you cook.

Built-ins & millwork

Bookcases, entertainment centers, window seats, and mudroom storage, built to fit oddly-shaped spaces exactly.

Bathroom vanities

Custom vanities finished to match or complement the rest of your home's cabinetry.

Closet systems

Built-in closet storage designed around what you actually own, not a one-size-fits-all kit.

Refacing & refinishing

Update existing cabinetry with new doors, paint, or hardware without a full teardown.

Design consultation

Not sure what you need yet? We'll walk your space with you and talk through options and budget.

Process

How a project comes together

1. Consultation

We visit your space, talk through your needs, and take measurements.

2. Design & quote

You get a detailed design and price before any work begins.

3. Build & install

We build in our shop and install on-site, with you kept in the loop throughout.
