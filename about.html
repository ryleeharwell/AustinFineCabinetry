About us

Built by hand, right here in Austin

Since 2009, Austin Fine Cabinetry has built custom cabinetry for kitchens, bathrooms, closets, and built-ins — with craftsmanship built to last.

Our story

Austin Fine Cabinetry has been building custom cabinetry since 2009. Our owner brings over two decades of experience in home construction, and that background shapes how we approach every project — with an eye for how cabinetry actually needs to perform in a real home, not just how it looks on install day.

We build everything in-house, using CNC woodworking technology to hold tight, consistent tolerances on every piece — so parts fit right the first time, and repeat orders come out the same every time. It's precision you can count on, backed by real craftsmanship behind it.

We're a local team, and we're genuinely passionate about woodworking and about building spaces that homeowners love living in. That's what keeps us building, project after project.
